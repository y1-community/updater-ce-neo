reported a successful flash of the "rockbox for y2" ROM. Device did NOT boot.

- LOGO: all zeros.
- boot1_readback.bin.gz: EMMC_BOOT header @0x0, BRLYT @0x200, ROM preloader byte-exact @0x800.
- lk/boot/recovery/secro: present but matched neither ROM at offset 0 (hashes in SHA256SUMS_not_committed;
  images themselves are too large / proprietary to commit).
- The same ROM flashed with SP Flash Tool (Windows) boots.
